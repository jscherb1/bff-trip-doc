Slide 3 — The problem we're solving

We Don't All Want the Same Vacation

The Nesters

Hang by the pool. Cook together. Let the kids run wild in a backyard that isn't ours. Nobody has to get in a car once we've landed.

The Explorers

Get out and see something new. Give the older kids a city to explore. Find a real restaurant that isn't the same four we always cook.

Most destinations make you pick a side. Ours shouldn't have to.

Slide 4 — Before we say where

What The Winning Destination Has To Do

Three non-negotiables, in the order we'd defend them. Wherever checks all three gets our vote.

01

Everyone gets here easily

Nonstop flights from every city in this group, into one major airport, with real rental car counters.

02

The heat isn't the dealbreaker people assume

Hot, yes. But nowhere close to the desert-in-July trip we all agreed to rule out — and every house has its own pool.

03

One house, two vacations

A resort-style property to disappear into, with a real city 30 to 55 minutes away when somebody wants out.

Pillar 03 — One house, two vacations

Stay In, or Go Out — Nobody Has to Agree

Stay in

  • Private pool, hot tub, and yard space for 9 kids to burn off energy
  • Cook as a group instead of splitting into four dinner reservations
  • Zero pants required, zero car seats buckled

Go out

  • Downtown Austin, Lady Bird Lake, live music — 30 to 55 minutes away
  • A real city with real restaurants, not just the closest chain
  • Enough for a half-day trip without committing the whole vacation to it

Every property we shortlisted sits in the Hill Country just outside the city — private and quiet, with Austin on standby.
